The Classy story

Born in the
salt air.

Classy Dishes started with a simple idea: that the best meals bring people closer. In 2018, our first little table opened a few steps from the Nyali shoreline, serving family recipes alongside flavours we picked up on travels.

Today, we are still a place for long lunches, barefoot sunsets, and plates that feel like a warm hello. Our kitchen is proudly Kenyan, endlessly curious, and always made for sharing.
